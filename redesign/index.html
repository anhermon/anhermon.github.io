Request a demo

Ideally an address on the site's own domain, so I can see you are connected to it.

  1. Request

    You send the site and consent.

    Your URL, a one-line goal and three tick-boxes: that you may ask, that I may email you, and, optionally, that the result may later be shown.

  2. Audit

    An objective audit by email, straight away.

    Lighthouse on mobile, HTTPS, viewport, page weight, stale copyright year and broken links. Measured, not opinion.

  3. Demo

    A private demo within about five days.

    The hero and one key section, redesigned and running at an unlisted address. It is deliberately limited: a sketch of a direction you can react to, not a finished site. See the before and after below.

  4. Build, optional

    The full build happens only on payment.

    $50 founding-rate landing page, 5 of 5 seats left. Other scopes on request. Until then the demo stays our work: no source or assets are handed over. See the scope.

  5. Case study, optional

    Shown publicly only with your explicit yes.

    The tick-box is a preference, not a permission. I ask again, with the finished piece in front of you, before anything goes public.

Terms and privacy, in short.

  • The demo is a private, unlisted prototype. It is not indexed and I do not share the address.
  • It stays our work. No source files or assets are handed over unless the full build is paid for.
  • I use only your public site content to prepare it.
  • I delete your submission and the demo on request: email me.
  • I do not contact you about anything else.
  • What is stored: the fields in the form, the consent boxes you ticked and the time, in a private database. No IP address.

The demo is made by an AI-agent pipeline that I direct, review and sign off.
