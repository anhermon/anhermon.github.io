What was built

Art direction and sign-off: Angel. Concept, copy, build, audio engine and QA: AI-agent pipeline, reviewed page by page.

A dark editorial direction: a Didone display serif at high contrast, a single sea photograph, mono labels borrowed from hardware makers, and a generative audio engine that only starts on a deliberate click.

  • Cinematic single-page launch site, static HTML/CSS/JS
  • WebAudio generative engine: four moods, muted until the first press
  • Audio analyser drives a light ribbon on the horizon, with a static fallback
  • Product object (dial and slab) drawn in CSS, plus three Codex-generated stills (the slab, a dial close-up, a room)
  • Specs table and a demo form that is deliberately non-submitting

Stack Static HTML, CSS, vanilla JS, WebAudio, canvas, self-hosted fonts, Cloudflare

Decisions, with the reasons.

Type

A Didone for the headline, a mono for the specs

Bodoni Moda (optical-size variable, roman and italic) carries the display lines, with the italic used for one word per heading. Instrument Sans handles body copy and Sometype Mono the small spec labels, which is how hardware brands tend to label things. Display tracking sits at -0.022 to -0.025em, as measured on the references.

Interaction

Signature interaction: sound only on request

The page is silent until the visitor presses Enter the room. A WebAudio engine then plays one of four moods (dusk, fog, rain, tide), and an analyser on the output drives the light ribbon on the horizon, so what you see is what you hear. A headless test taps the audio output to assert it is silent before the press and audible after.

How it was started Eight reference sites were measured first (hardware, audio and editorial brands). Takeaways that shaped it: dark grounds near #08090a, display tracking -0.022 to -0.025em, section padding up to 160px, a single easing curve at 0.7s, reveals at 0.3-0.5s.

Honest limits.

  • The hero sea and three more stills (the slab, a dial macro, a room) are AI-generated; the interactive dial and slab are drawn in CSS.
  • The audio was verified by an automated output tap (29 checks) and by ear on a laptop, not on a physical phone. The iOS silent switch mutes WebAudio, and the sound layer is procedural: a sampled one was blocked by an API permission.
  • A concept: nothing for sale, nothing stored. Kerning on the hero headline and the horizon line were fixed after review; check the live preview for the current state.
