Every automated step has a human check.

  • Measures reference sites for type scale, spacing and motionI choose which references count and what to ignore
  • Drafts the copy and generates the pageI check every claim and number against its source
  • Renders at 1440 and 390 pixels, runs LighthouseI look at the screenshots, not only the scores
  • A second, separate agent critiques each sectionI decide what is generic and send it back
  • Prepares the files for handoffI sign off before anything is delivered or published

Agents Angel. The brief, the audience and the direction you choose are mine and yours from the start.

What that means for you.

Speed
Days, not weeks.A first full draft in a day or two, because agents do the typing. My review is the part that cannot be rushed.
Cost
Lower, not cut corners.The production work is automated. That is why the founding rate is what it is, and why it will rise.
Revisions
One consolidated round.Agents re-render fast, but each round still costs my review time, so a fixed-price page has one list of changes.
Mistakes
Mine to own.Agents can state things that are wrong. You supply the facts, I check what I can, and nothing unverified goes out. If something is wrong, that is my responsibility.

What you will never see here.

  • Client logos, testimonials or numbers I cannot show the source for
  • A concept presented as client work: every concept is labelled CONCEPT
  • An unreviewed agent sending messages to you in my name
  • A work piece published without my explicit approval of that piece
